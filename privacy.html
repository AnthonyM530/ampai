Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

AMP AI makes AI pictures and videos from the photos and text you give it. To do that, it sends them to the AI companies named below. This policy explains what we collect, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

Who we are

AMP AI is provided by Anthony Magdaleno ("we", "us"). You can reach us at ampsupport247@gmail.com.

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location or your advertising ID. The app has no ads and no analytics tools that follow what you do in it, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To make the creations you ask for and keep them in your library.
  • To run purchases, credits and refunds.
  • To keep the service safe: we check prompts and photos against our rules, act on reports and block accounts that break the rules.
  • To fix problems, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal duties.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising. We do not train our own AI models on your photos or creations.

Photos of faces

Photos you add may show faces. We use them only to make the creation you asked for and to check them against our content rules. We do not use them to identify you or anyone else, and we do not build a database of faces.

Before you add your first photo, the app asks for your permission to store it and to send it to the AI providers below. If you add a photo of another person, you must have that person’s permission first.

Who can open your files

Creations have public links. Each creation is stored at its own web link. The link is long and hard to guess, but it is not private: anyone who has the link can open the creation without signing in, until you delete it. Share a link only with people you want to see it. We plan to make these links private in a later update.

Photos you add are private. They are kept in private storage and can be opened only through links that expire after one hour. The app makes those links for you and for the AI provider that makes your creation.

If you stop using AMP AI and do not delete your account, we delete your photos and creations no later than 3 years after your last use.

When we delete something, a copy at an AI provider or in a network cache may remain for a time under that provider’s own rules.

Your choices

  • Delete a creation: open it in My Creations and tap the trash. This removes the creation. The photos you added for it stay until you delete your account.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This removes your account, your photos, your creations and your credit history. It does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in your Apple account settings. The records listed above as kept after deletion stay.
  • Your account lives on your phone: if you delete the app without deleting your account first, we can no longer connect you to that account, and its photos and creations stay on our servers until the limit for unused accounts above. Use Delete Account before you delete the app.
  • Withdraw your permission for AI processing: turn off AI Processing in Settings. You won’t be able to add photos, enhance prompts or create until you turn it on again. Photos you already added stay until you delete your account.
  • Get a copy of your data, or correct it: email us at the address above.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it and to have it deleted. Email us to use them. We will not treat you differently for doing so.

Children

AMP AI is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. Do not add photos of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app or appears in a creation, email us and we will remove the data.

Where your data is processed

We store data in the United States. AI providers may process it in other countries. AMP AI is not offered in the European Union.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and limit who can reach it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that your data will never be accessed without permission.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we share your photos, the app will ask for your permission again.
