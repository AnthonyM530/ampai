Common questions

My creation failed. Did I lose credits?
No. When a creation fails, its credits come back to your balance automatically, usually within minutes. A stuck creation can take up to an hour to fail and return them. If they still haven’t come back, email us.
I bought credits and they haven’t arrived.
Credits can take a few minutes to appear; the app keeps checking for about ten minutes. If the balance is still wrong after that, email us the order ID. Restore Purchases will not add them.
What does Restore Purchases do?
It brings back an active subscription, for example on a new phone. It does not add credits.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then AMP AI, then Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
Can I get a refund?
Apple takes all payments and decides refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a purchase, the credits it added are taken back.
What happens if I delete the app or change phones?
Your account lives only on your phone. If you delete the app, change phones or reset the phone, the account and its credits and creations cannot be recovered. An active subscription can be brought back with Restore Purchases.
How do I delete a creation?
Open it in My Creations and tap the trash.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This removes your account, photos, creations and credit balance. Cancel any subscription separately in your Apple account settings. Do this before you delete the app.
Who can see my creations?
Only people who have a creation’s link. The link is hard to guess but not private, so share it with care. The photos you add are private.
How do I report a creation?
Tap the flag on a creation or on a template and pick a reason, or email us the link or a description.
Someone used my photo without asking.
Email us the link or a description. We will remove it.
How do I stop my photos going to AI providers?
Turn off AI Processing in Settings. You won’t be able to add photos, enhance prompts or create while it is off. Photos you already added stay until you delete your account.
How do I ask about my data?
Email us. See the Privacy Policy for what we keep and for how long.

Content rules

AMP AI has no tolerance for content that breaks these rules or for accounts that abuse the service. Do not use AMP AI to make or share anything that:

  • shows a real person who has not agreed to it;
  • shows anyone under 18;
  • is sexual or shows nudity;
  • harasses, threatens, defames or demeans a person, or promotes hatred or violence;
  • pretends to be someone in order to deceive, or misleads people about real events, including elections;
  • infringes anyone’s copyright, trademark, privacy or publicity rights;
  • breaks the law.

Creations are AI-made. Do not present one as a real photo or recording of a real event.

We check prompts and photos against these rules and may refuse a request. A request refused by these checks is not charged. You can report a creation or a template in the app, or by email. We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules and block accounts that make it.

If you appear in a creation and did not agree to it, email us the link or a description. We will remove it.
